About

From physics to business decisions

I chose a B.Sc. in Physics at Banaras Hindu University over engineering. Physics taught me to reason from first principles: build a simple model of a system, test it against data and accept what the data says.

I'm now doing an MBA in Business Analytics at BITS Pilani, applying the same approach to business problems. Every project here follows that discipline. I state the business question, use real data, check the method, and report findings that hold up, including when the honest answer is that a model doesn't beat the simple benchmark.

I'm most interested in roles where analytics turns into decisions: credit and fraud risk, market and FX risk, and data-driven product management.

FX strategy · Forecasting & hedging

USD/INR Outlook & Hedging Backtest

A strategy note on the rupee using FRED data from 2019 to 2026. The rupee weakens steadily on trend, but ARIMA forecasts are no better than a random walk with drift in a 24-origin walk-forward test. A backtest of forwards priced with covered interest parity measures what hedging was worth to a $1M-a-month importer.
