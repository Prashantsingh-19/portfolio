# About Prashant Singh

## Who Prashant is
Prashant Singh is an MBA candidate in Business Analytics at BITS Pilani (2025–2027). Before the MBA he completed a B.Sc. in Physics at Banaras Hindu University (BHU), 2020–2023. Most recently he was a Product Management Intern at i3digital health, a health-tech company, from May to July 2026, where he worked on an oncology-focused AI product.

## What Prashant works on
Prashant works where analytics turns into business decisions. His projects cover credit and fraud risk (scorecards, default prediction, explainable fraud detection), market risk and volatility (GARCH, VaR and Expected Shortfall, Monte Carlo), business analytics (customer retention, IT service SLA risk, project delay risk, market sizing) and Gen AI products (RAG assistants and agentic copilots). He is open to full-time roles in risk analytics, data science and product management.

## Why a physics graduate chose an MBA
Prashant fell in love with physics while preparing for engineering entrance exams, so much that after clearing a few colleges he dropped engineering and pursued Physics instead. What drew him to physics is how it lets you imagine, for example how a moving electron generates a magnetic field, while its rules and constraints make an idea hold up in the real world. With a foundation in first principles, he wanted to point that thinking at business rather than machines and see whether it could create real impact. That question led him to an MBA in Business Analytics at BITS Pilani rather than an M.Sc. or a PhD. The best way to find out was to start doing it, and his projects are the result.

## How Prashant works
Prashant starts every project from a business question, uses real data where possible, checks the method, and reports findings honestly, including when a model does not beat a simple benchmark. In his AI products he follows one design rule: code computes the numbers and the language model only writes the words, with a validator that blocks made-up figures.
