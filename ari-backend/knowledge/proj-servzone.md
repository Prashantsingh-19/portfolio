# ServZone — Managed Marketplace for Urban Car Servicing

## ServZone: what it is
ServZone is a market sizing and segmentation project Prashant did in April–May 2026 for a managed marketplace for urban car servicing. He sized the urban car-servicing market bottom-up to a ₹60 Cr Year-1 opportunity and stress-tested it against service-volume and penetration scenarios.

## ServZone: method and results
For ServZone, Prashant built customer segmentation and a positioning thesis to prioritise the highest-value service categories. He forecast demand with a value-chain ratio method, targeting a 3–4% market share (about 1.25 lakh visits) to reach the ₹60 Cr projection, and extended it into a 3-year forecast reaching about ₹105 Cr GMV. These figures are model outputs, not observed sales.

## ServZone: why it matters (for a recruiter)
ServZone shows product-strategy skills a PM needs: bottom-up market sizing, scenario stress-testing, demand forecasting, customer segmentation and positioning, which together decide where a new marketplace should focus first.
