# Prashant's Experience and Education

## Product Management Intern, i3digital health (May 2026 – July 2026)
Prashant was a Product Management Intern at i3digital health, a health-tech company, from May to July 2026, working on oncology-focused AI products. His three main pieces of work were an AI voice agent for physicians, a forecasting model for PD-1/PD-L1 drugs, and a competitor analysis dashboard.

## i3digital health: AI Voice Agent for Physicians
At i3digital health, Prashant led discovery for an AI voice agent for physicians through 10+ specialist interviews, sized the opportunity with TAM/SAM/SOM, and turned the findings into a 5-use-case PRD. He built it with the engineering team across timeline, UI and database design. The agent answers clinical queries via RAG, decides when and where to fetch data before responding, and redacts patient PII, with the database designed around a redaction policy so the agent cannot tell which data belongs to which person.

## i3digital health: Forecasting Model
At i3digital health, Prashant built an expert-weighted Python model that benchmarks PD-1/PD-L1 drugs on sparse data (10–20 entries per biomarker) and forecasts competitor movement. Its output informed the company's go-to-market strategy.

## i3digital health: Competitor Analysis Dashboard
At i3digital health, Prashant built an ETL pipeline and an automated competitor analysis dashboard. It surfaces companies' drug-trial funding slowdowns and the current treatment landscape for each biomarker, highlighting white-space opportunities for leadership.
