## i3digital health: discovery and PRD for a voice agent
During the i3digital health internship, Prashant led discovery for a RAG-based agentic voice agent for physicians. He ran a competitive analysis of more than 10 companies and interviewed specialists, then wrote a PRD covering 5 use cases, with patient PII redaction built in by design. ## i3digital health: oncology competitive-intelligence pipeline
