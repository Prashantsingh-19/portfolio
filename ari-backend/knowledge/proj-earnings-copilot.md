# Earnings Intelligence Copilot — Agentic RAG over Earnings Calls

## Earnings Intelligence Copilot: what it is
Earnings Intelligence Copilot is a Generative AI and NLP project Prashant built from July to October 2026. It is a retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) pipeline over 30 earnings-call transcripts across 3 sectors that extracts and scores management guidance on capital expenditure and margins, so analysts can find and compare what management said with the source evidence behind it.

## Earnings Intelligence Copilot: how it's built
Prashant engineered chunking, embedding and dense retrieval in Python, with a tool-calling agent layer on top. It follows an agent-to-data pattern for governed enterprise question answering: the agent decides which tools and data to query, and answers stay traceable to transcript excerpts rather than unsupported summaries. It also supports comparing guidance across companies and uploading new transcript collections.

## Earnings Intelligence Copilot: why it matters (for a recruiter)
The project shows hands-on Gen AI engineering (RAG, embeddings, agents) applied to a real analyst workflow, with a focus on traceability and governance. It is a portfolio project, not investment advice.
